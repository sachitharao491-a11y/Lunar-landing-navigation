About

Lunar Moon Navigator is an educational demo for exploring lunar sites, comparing landing candidates and sketching rover routes.

The interactive globe uses NASA Goddard's LRO imagery and topography model. Site values remain illustrative samples, not live NASA recommendations. Model: NASA SVS Moon 3D Models. Backend, database, accounts and PDF export are not part of this single-page build; reports are plain text. Distances use a sphere of radius 1,737.4 km.
