About

Lunar Moon Navigator is an educational demo for exploring lunar sites, comparing landing candidates and sketching rover routes.

The interactive globe uses NASA Goddard's LRO imagery and topography model. Site values remain illustrative samples, not live NASA recommendations. Model: NASA SVS Moon 3D Models. When served by the FastAPI backend, sites come from PostGIS and accounts sync favorites and plans. Distances use a sphere of radius 1,737.4 km.
