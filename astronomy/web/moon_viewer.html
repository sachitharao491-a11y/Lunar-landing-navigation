Lunar coordinate viewer

Set a lunar site and UTC time. The red marker shows the site; Sun and Earth rays use their local azimuth and elevation. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.

Model mapping: 0° longitude is placed on the model’s +Z meridian; east longitude rotates toward +X. The GLB does not include a verified lunar prime-meridian orientation, so this shows the coordinate mapping on the sphere, not a certified geographic overlay.
